## __Methodology__

**Resident rat reports** include only Rat Sighting and Condition Attracting Rodents. Signs of Rodents is treated separately as inspector-recorded activity, while Mouse Sighting is excluded from the rat metric.

The **Cross-Dataset Gap Index** compares each ZIP’s percentile rank for resident rat reports per 100 restaurants with its percentile rank for distinct restaurant inspection events per 100 restaurants.

A ZIP is **Scoreable** only when it contains at least 200 resident rat reports and 20 distinct restaurants. ZIPs below either threshold remain visible, but receive no score.

Restaurant inspections are a proxy for recorded activity in commercial food establishments—not documented responses to individual 311 reports. ZIP codes are geographic reporting areas, not neighborhoods. **_A high index identifies a cross-dataset mismatch_** for further analysis; it does not prove neglect, inadequate service, or rat prevalence.

Administrative closure does not prove investigation or resolution. Closure behavior changed abruptly around April 2026, so closure timing is analyzed separately and is not included in the index.

**Data coverage**
311 records: Jan 1, 2025 – Sep 17, 2026
Restaurant inspections: Jan 2, 2025 – Sep 16, 2026
